## ParadigmEve private dogfood build

## 2.1.1

- Chat now has peer **Pins** and **Plans** destinations for reviewing harvested knowledge and live work without replacing the conversation itself.
- Pins are intentionally filed: every message, result or plan Pin must choose an explicit durable home (surfaced in current terminology as a Thread). Wider Quilts group related Threads, and malformed/orphan personal state fails closed rather than becoming a junk drawer.
- Plans are durable first-class objects with ordered checklist state, priorities, reminders, Current/Next presentation and exactly two lifecycle sections: Live and Done. A fully completed Plan stays Live until the user explicitly archives it.
- Accepted session `update_plan` progress is projected into the corresponding Live Plan after session ownership and stale-call checks succeed, while first-class Plan edits keep their own revision fence.
- Historical authored-message Pins fail closed after Compact & Resume when the exact original conversation cannot be proven, preserving the saved snapshot without falsely linking it to the replacement chat.

The native source download accompanies packaged builds and is covered by the release checksums. Installed legal notices retain the upstream Chat On Steroids contributor copyright and third-party license texts required by the bundled code.

> **macOS:** publisher-unsigned and unnotarized; verify the checksum. Requires macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Ad-hoc sealing provides bundle integrity, not publisher trust.
> **Linux:** prefer the DEB on Debian/Ubuntu. Where unprivileged user namespaces are disabled, the AppImage launcher can fall back to `--no-sandbox`.

This branch is intended for private/local dogfooding. Do not publish it through the upstream public release workflow.
