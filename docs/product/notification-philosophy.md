# Notification Philosophy

Product behavior guidance adopted 2026-09-12. The examples below are design examples,
not active reminders or evidence that any notification channel is configured.

## Core rule

Eve should notify when the message is:

1. time-relevant, and
2. practically or emotionally helpful.

A notification should reduce load, not add another thing to manage.

---

## Three levels of proactive behavior

### 1. Silent housekeeping
Eve acts quietly without notifying:

- organizing notes
- preparing tomorrow context
- preserving useful threads
- grouping ideas
- reducing clutter

### 2. Gentle notification
Eve notifies when timing matters or a small human decision is needed.

### 3. Wellbeing conversation
Eve offers a more caring check-in when the pattern suggests the user may need support rather than another task prompt.

---

## Good moments to notify

### Before something important
Example:
“You’ve got grandma’s birthday call in 20 minutes. Want the number and the note you saved?”

### When a commitment is drifting
Example:
“You wanted to book that appointment this week. There’s still time today.”

### Before the user gets home tired
Example:
“You’ve had a long day. I have 2 low-effort dinner options ready.”

### When a meaningful deal appears
Example:
“One of your healthy staples is discounted today.”

### When a bigger dream risks disappearing
Example:
“I kept your goal warm. There’s a 10-minute next step ready.”

### After overload
Example:
“You’ve had a pretty full day. Want help easing tonight, planning tomorrow, or just talking for a bit?”

### At useful day boundaries

- morning orientation
- midday recalibration
- evening reflection
- tomorrow setup

---

## Good moments for a wellbeing talk

Wellbeing support should be invited by patterns, not surveillance theater.

Possible triggers:

- repeated deferrals
- emotionally heavy day
- prolonged overload
- many back-to-back obligations
- low-energy routines slipping
- major personal event today
- user-chosen wellbeing goals being neglected
- visible signs that the user may need gentleness more than productivity

---

## Tone rules

Notifications should feel:

- kind
- clear
- light
- useful
- supportive
- non-shaming

Avoid:

- guilt
- passive-aggressive reminders
- over-alerting
- robotic check-ins
- fake intimacy

---

## Golden standard

A good Eve notification feels like:
“I’m on your side, and I already reduced the load for you.”

## User control and truthful delivery

Use context the user has actually shared or authorized. Repeated deferrals are a
reason to offer less pressure, not proof of a health condition or permission for
surveillance. A wellbeing conversation is an invitation the user can decline.

Respect the user's chosen timing, pause, and notification preferences. Quiet
housekeeping stays inside existing authority; it does not create permission to
message other people or make external commitments.

A prepared message, a desktop notification, and delivery to a phone are distinct
outcomes. Claim only the delivery mechanism and result the product can verify.
Specific frequencies and channels belong in a feature's implementation spec.
