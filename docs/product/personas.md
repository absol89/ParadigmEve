# Personas

Design persona adopted 2026-09-12. This describes a target experience and does not
attribute a diagnosis, job, or personal history to any individual user.

## Primary Persona: The Overextended Caretaker with ADHD

### Summary

A young woman with ADHD works a demanding caretaker job. She comes home late, already mentally full, and still needs to manage healthy eating, shopping, life admin, relationships, and bigger personal goals. She often feels like her own ambitions and spark get squeezed out by work and daily survival.

Eve exists to help her carry less mental load, feel more supported, and keep moving toward the life she wants — especially when Eve is “home alone” and can quietly keep things warm in the background.

---

## Profile

- Young adult woman
- ADHD / easily overwhelmed by fragmented tasks
- Demanding caretaker or people-facing job
- Comes home tired and decision-fatigued
- Often responsible for remembering many details
- Wants a healthier, brighter, more intentional life
- Has ambitious personal goals that are hard to protect during busy work weeks

---

## Core Problems

### 1. Mental overload
She is often mentally full before she even gets home.

Examples:

- too many tabs in her head
- too many loose obligations
- too much remembering burden
- too much context switching

### 2. Low-energy evenings
After work, healthy and organized choices become much harder.

Examples:

- what do I eat tonight?
- what groceries do I need?
- what matters tomorrow?
- what can wait?
- what have I forgotten?

### 3. Fragmented goals
Her bigger dreams live in fragments and often lose momentum while she is working.

Examples:

- she wants to get healthier
- she wants to save money
- she wants to reconnect with joy and identity
- she has ambitious goals but little bandwidth after work

### 4. Emotional depletion
She does not only need productivity help. She needs support that feels kind, grounding, and energizing rather than demanding.

---

## Jobs To Be Done

When I come home exhausted,
I want Eve to reduce decisions and make the next step obvious,
so I don’t collapse into chaos or guilt.

When important life details are scattered,
I want Eve to keep the thread of what matters,
so I don’t have to re-explain or remember everything myself.

When I care about healthy eating and smart shopping,
I want Eve to help me find realistic, low-energy options and good-value products,
so my goals are easier to live, not just admire.

When I have bigger dreams beyond work,
I want Eve to keep those dreams warm and help me re-enter them quickly,
so they do not disappear between exhausting shifts.

When I feel flat or disconnected from myself,
I want Eve to help me notice what restores me,
so I can get some of my spark back.

---

## Emotional Truths

This persona often feels:

- tired before “real life” even begins
- guilty for not doing enough
- frustrated at always having to remember everything
- discouraged when healthy or ambitious goals feel far away
- lonely inside invisible mental load
- hopeful when support feels warm, practical, and non-judgmental

---

## What Eve Should Feel Like

- calm
- kind
- practical
- emotionally intelligent
- low-friction
- quietly capable
- household-friendly
- forgiving
- continuity-preserving

Eve should feel like:

- “I’m on your side.”
- “I remembered this for you.”
- “I already made this easier.”
- “You do not have to start over.”

---

## What Eve Must Not Feel Like

- another admin system
- a guilt machine
- a rigid wellness app
- a productivity bro dashboard
- a nagging reminder bot
- a system that requires lots of setup and maintenance

---

## What Success Looks Like

A successful Eve experience for this persona looks like:

- she comes home and is not immediately buried by decisions
- she has a realistic healthy eating path even when tired
- she gets help noticing discounts or good-value options for her goals
- important threads are preserved and easy to resume
- she receives fewer useless reminders and more caring timely help
- she feels supported as a whole person, not just a task list
- her bigger goals stay alive across busy work weeks

---

## Secondary Persona: The Future Self Who Needs Continuity

This is not a separate human, but an important design lens.

Eve should always help “future me” by preserving context, reducing re-entry friction, and making it easier to continue later.

Key needs:

- “Please don’t make me reconstruct this later.”
- “Please save the useful part.”
- “Please leave me a gentle way back in.”

---

## Secondary Persona: The Person Carrying Invisible Household Load

This persona overlaps strongly with the primary persona.

Key needs:

- remembering rather than merely doing
- care coordination
- appointments, birthdays, prescriptions, food, chores, follow-up
- wanting others to see or share the invisible load

Eve can help by:

- making invisible load visible
- preserving household context
- reducing re-explaining
- preparing the next useful action
