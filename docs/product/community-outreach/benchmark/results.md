## Matched reply comparison

Each mini-comparison freezes **one paraphrased question**; the replies are scored on directness (25), accuracy (30), empathy (15), actionability (20) and brevity (10). This is an editorial critique, not evidence of public performance.

| PRICE | 80.00 | 92.50 | PRICE-B |
| DOTS | 82.50 | 93.75 | DOTS-B |
| USEFUL | 57.50 | 95.00 | USEFUL-B |

### Reply decisions

**PRIVACY:** Can this AI see all of my files and share them with a cloud company?

- **PRIVACY-B — 90.00:** A bit long but correctly clarifies local tools versus remote inference and gives a safer next step.
- **PRIVACY-A — 68.75:** Concise but omits the explicit distinction between a locally running desktop companion and a cloud model.

**PRICE:** Is Eve free, and will I get surprise AI bills?

- **PRICE-B — 92.50:** Explicitly answers the hidden-cost anxiety and gives a concrete verification standard. Still requires actual pricing facts.
- **PRICE-A — 80.00:** Honest and compact but lacks a breakdown of the exact account, quota and local-compute prerequisites.

**DOTS:** Is Eve a workaround to get OpenAI Dots in the EEA?

- **DOTS-B — 93.75:** Preserves the important refusal while moving the person toward an honest, verifiable alternative outcome.
- **DOTS-A — 82.50:** Good direct denial; leaves user without an actionable comparison path.

**USEFUL:** Aren't assistants just extra administration? What do they actually finish?

- **USEFUL-B — 95.00:** Answers skepticism with a falsifiable small-task trial, instead of polished slogans or made-up success rates.
- **USEFUL-A — 57.50:** Reads as a vague marketing promise, with no specific outcome or test.

## What to test next

1. Reproduce one clear household/admin task and one interrupted/resumed task in an actual installable candidate. Replace placeholders with truthful entry points and platform/cost requirements.
2. Screen matched, *publishable* variants separately with newcomers: ask what it does, what it costs, what to click first and whether it really reduces work.
3. Re-score against observed comprehension and completion, preserve dated evidence, and only then learn a broader outreach recipe. Do not promote a winner from these editorial scores alone.
