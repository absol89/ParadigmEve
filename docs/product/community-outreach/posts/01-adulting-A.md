## Copy

The annoying part of adulting isn't always the big thing.

It's the school form you meant to read. The appointment you have to compare. The folder full of stuff you *will* sort out one day.

I've been building **Eve** around a small idea: instead of handing you a prettier to-do list, an assistant should help get the boring parts handled.

Our test is simple: start with **one** task you've been putting off, see what Eve can actually prepare or finish, and keep the decisions that only you can make.

I'm working on a short, honest walkthrough that shows what works, what still needs your approval and how to start. What would be your first task?
