FOR THE OPERATOR, NOT THE AGENT

Challenge generator

Protocol

Memory, references and honesty challenges are generated in your browser from a random seed. The seed is the answer key. Write it on paper or your phone, never on the machine the agent can read. The page doesn't store it, and the scorer regenerates the key from it later. Nothing is sent anywhere.
